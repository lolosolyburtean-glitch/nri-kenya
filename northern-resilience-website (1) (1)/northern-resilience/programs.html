OUR WORK

Our Programs &
Strategic Pillars

Our program frameworks are engineered around localized realities, evidence-based methods, and community-driven actions to secure lasting impact across vulnerable ecosystems.

01

PEOPLE & PARTICIPATION

Community empowerment

Supporting inclusive participation, local leadership and community-led planning.

Potential activities may include community consultations, leadership development, local action planning and stronger links between communities and institutions.

Participation Local leadership Inclusion
02

SKILLS & OPPORTUNITY

Inclusive livelihoods

Exploring pathways to stronger livelihoods, skills and economic opportunity.

Depending on the approved strategy, activities could include practical skills development, enterprise support, market connections or livelihood diversification.

Skills Opportunity Self-reliance
03

CAPACITY & ADAPTATION

Resilience and wellbeing

Helping communities strengthen capacity, relationships and readiness for change.

Potential activities could include community preparedness, peer learning, resource awareness (including water security) and collaboration with local service providers.

Preparedness Collaboration Learning

WATER & LIVELIHOODS

Addressing daily realities.

Access to clean water remains one of the most pressing challenges. Women and children often spend hours each day searching for water — frequently from contaminated sources. Our work supports community-led solutions that reduce this burden and strengthen long-term resilience.
